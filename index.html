Coral AI › AI Presence

AI presence

Figma link Developer doc

AI presence refers to the visual and interactive signals that communicate when, where and how AI is involved in a user experience. It encompasses the symbol, iconography, disclosure patterns, and provenance labeling used across Sea products.

Original representation

The Coral AI symbol is the single unified mark that represents AI across all Sea products. It is derived from the letter "A" and rendered as a gradient form — signalling intelligence, warmth, and forward motion.

Symbol

A gradient "A" letterform built from two overlapping strokes. The gradient runs from violet (#746DFF) to coral (#F7716D), representing the spectrum of AI capabilities.

Accessibility

The AI symbol must always have a text alternative when used as a standalone icon. Use aria-label="AI" or pair with visible text. Never rely on colour alone to communicate AI status.

Anatomy

1. Gradient fill

The diagonal gradient from #746DFF (violet) to #F7716D (coral) is the signature of Coral AI. Always use this gradient — never a flat colour version in product contexts.

2. Letterform

The "A" shape is built from two angled strokes and one horizontal crossbar. The proportions are fixed — do not stretch, skew, or reweight.

3. Container

When placed inside a container (button, badge, chip), use a rounded rectangle with radius proportional to size. For standalone icon-only usage, the container may be omitted.

Discovery type 1 — subtle

Type 1 discovery is the lowest-friction way to surface AI. The AI symbol appears inline or as a small badge — it does not interrupt the user's flow and is only revealed when contextually appropriate.

When to use

Use when AI functionality is secondary or optional. Suitable for inline suggestions, hover states, and contextual tooltips. The user is not required to interact.

Behaviour

The symbol appears on hover or after a delay. On mobile, it appears after a long-press or as a persistent but small badge. Always dismissible.

Size

Icon only, 20×20px or 16×16px. No label required. Pair with a tooltip on hover to describe the AI capability.

Discovery type 2 — prominent

Type 2 discovery is a more prominent, proactive entry point. It is used when AI is a primary feature of the surface, or when onboarding users to a new AI capability for the first time.

When to use

Use when AI is a key value proposition of the page or flow. Typical contexts include AI-first screens, onboarding, feature announcements, and empty states that benefit from AI suggestions.

Behaviour

Always visible — not hidden behind hover. May include a label, description, or call-to-action. Clicking opens the AI feature directly.

Size

Icon 28×28px or 40×40px with an optional text label. Use the contained (gradient background) version. Ensure adequate touch target of at least 44×44px.

Provenance

Provenance labeling tells users where AI-generated content came from. It builds trust, supports transparency, and meets regulatory requirements. Always label AI-generated content — never present it as if it were authored by a human.

General context

Inline label

Use a small "AI" badge or the Coral AI symbol inline with the content it labels. Place it consistently — either always before or always after the content block.

Footer attribution

For longer AI-generated content blocks, use a footer row with the AI symbol and a short attribution string such as "Generated by AI" or "AI summary".

AI Summary

The sprint goal is focused on improving onboarding. Three key tasks were identified: redesign the welcome screen, simplify sign-up, and add contextual help.

Consistently when to use

01
Always label AI-generated content

Any text, image, ranking, summary or recommendation that was produced or significantly altered by AI must be labeled. No exceptions.

Mandatory All surfaces
02
Use the official symbol consistently

Only use the Coral AI gradient "A" symbol — never replace it with generic robot icons, sparkles, or other AI metaphors. Consistency builds user recognition across products.

Symbol only No substitutes
03
Do not use AI presence for non-AI features

Do not attach the AI symbol to algorithmic or rule-based features that do not use a machine learning model. This dilutes trust and misleads users.

ML only No misuse
04
Scale discovery level to feature importance

Use Type 1 (subtle) for background or assistive AI. Use Type 2 (prominent) only when AI is the primary purpose of the screen or interaction.

Type 1 → assistive Type 2 → primary
05
Provide a way to learn more

Every AI label should be tappable or hoverable to reveal a short explanation of what data was used and why the AI produced this result. This is especially important for decisions that affect users.

AI Button

Figma link Developer doc

The AI Button is the primary entry point for triggering AI-powered actions. It visually signals AI capability through the Coral AI star icon and gradient styling.

Anatomy

1. Icon

The Coral AI star icon (✦) identifies this as an AI action. Always use the official icon — do not substitute.

2. Label (optional)

A short verb-led label clarifies the action. Omit in space-constrained contexts where the icon alone is sufficient.

3. Container

The gradient background signals AI capability. Available in contained, outlined, and plain styles.
